The process

Start with your visibility gaps.

  1. Discovery: Review your audience, existing memberships, sales priorities, and capacity.
  2. Representation plan: Agree on activities, messaging, responsibilities, and follow-up.
  3. Show up and connect: Represent your brand consistently and create appropriate introductions.
  4. Review: Assess activity, conversations, next steps, and adjustments.
