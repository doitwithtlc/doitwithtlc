  1. Sign in to GitHub with your doitwithtlc account.
  2. Open an event draft and check its original source, dates, times and location.
  3. To correct details, edit the draft before approval. Unconfirmed events stay in review.
  4. Comment /approve to publish, or /reject to decline.
  5. Wait for the publication confirmation. Failed publication leaves the draft open.

Drafts in this queue are public event information. Personal details submitted through the website form stay in the CRM; that form is a separate intake route.
