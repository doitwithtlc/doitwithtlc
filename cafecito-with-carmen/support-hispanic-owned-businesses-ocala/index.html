Culture & community

Supporting Hispanic-owned businesses starts with a relationship

By The Liaison Companies · Cafecito with Carmen · November 5, 2026

Leer el resumen en español ↓

Supporting a Hispanic-owned business can begin with a purchase. It can also begin with a question: who is behind this business, and what are they building?

Culture creates a starting point for connection. The relationship grows when we learn what someone offers, respect their work, and understand who they can best serve. A shared language can help open that conversation, but you do not have to speak Spanish to participate.

Get to know the business beyond the label

Ask the owner what they want people to know about their work. Find out which services they provide, where they work, and how they prefer to receive inquiries. Avoid assuming that every Hispanic-owned business has the same story, audience, or language preference.

Make your recommendation useful

When someone asks for a recommendation, explain why the business could be a fit. Share a direct website or business profile and describe your own experience accurately. If you have not hired them, say how you know them. A clear introduction gives both people something to work with.

Keep showing up

Support can include paying on time, respecting the price, leaving an honest review after a purchase, or inviting an owner into a relevant conversation. Ask permission before sharing someone’s personal contact information or making an introduction.

For a business owner, a useful next step is to choose one local business this week, learn what they need, and look for a specific way to connect them with an opportunity.

Resumen en español

Apoyar a los negocios hispanos comienza con una relación

Apoyar a un negocio hispano puede empezar con una compra, una recomendación o una conversación. Pregunta qué ofrece, a quién sirve y cómo prefiere recibir consultas. Cada negocio tiene su propia historia; no debemos asumir su idioma ni sus necesidades.

Si lo recomiendas, explica cómo lo conoces y comparte información pública del negocio. Si todavía no has utilizado sus servicios, dilo. Pide permiso antes de compartir datos personales o presentar a dos personas.

No tienes que hablar español para crear una relación. Empieza con interés, respeto y una acción concreta: conocer a un negocio local y buscar una oportunidad relevante para conectarlo.
