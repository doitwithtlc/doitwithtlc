Small business & community

Supporting Hispanic-owned businesses takes more than a like

By Carmen Garcia Tirado · November 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Leer el resumen en español ↓

We can cheer for a small business online and still forget to mention it when someone actually needs what it sells. That gap is where I want us to do better.

For Hispanic-owned businesses in Ocala and Marion County, support can be practical: learn what the business does, understand who it serves, and make the next connection easier. Culture can start the conversation. Quality, service, and follow-through give the relationship somewhere to go.

A timely reason to start

The U.S. Small Business Administration identifies November 28, 2026, as Small Business Saturday. Its official page also links to free promotional materials for business owners. See the SBA’s date and resources.

My take: use that day as an invitation to discover someone new, then keep the relationship going after the holiday shopping season. Our local businesses deserve consideration throughout the year.

Make your recommendation useful

“They’re great” is kind. “They do this work, serve this area, and here is how to request an estimate” gives someone a next step.

  • Ask the owner which product, service, or customer need they want people to understand.
  • Share a current business link and explain what the business actually offers.
  • If you have used the service, describe your own experience honestly. If you have only met the owner, make that clear.
  • Ask before connecting people or passing along private contact information.

Give expertise room to speak

I want Hispanic-owned businesses considered for the work they are qualified to do: projects, professional services, speaking opportunities, vendor spaces, and partnerships. Let’s learn their expertise alongside their story.

That also means asking questions instead of making assumptions about language, background, or price. Every owner gets to define their own business.

Your next move

Choose one local business this week. Learn what it needs to be known for. Then take one relevant action: buy something you need, share a useful recommendation, or make an introduction both people welcome.

Small business support becomes meaningful when someone can use it.

Resumen en español

Apoyar a los negocios hispanos requiere acciones concretas: conocer sus servicios, compartir información actualizada y recomendar con honestidad. Small Business Saturday será el 28 de noviembre de 2026, según la SBA. Aprovechemos esa fecha para descubrir negocios y mantener la relación durante todo el año.
