Community & visibility

The room matters. So does what you bring to it.

By Carmen Garcia Tirado · November 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Leer el resumen en español ↓

A full calendar can make you busy. I think a thoughtful calendar can make you useful.

For a small business in Marion County, being present means more when you understand why a room matters: who is there, what they need, and where you can contribute.

Choose a purpose before choosing a booth

My recommendation is to begin with the people you want to understand. A professional gathering, family festival, and nonprofit project each create different opportunities and expectations.

Before buying a vendor space or sponsorship, ask the organizer who the event is designed for, what participation includes, and what responsibilities you will have. Look for a sensible fit with your service, time, and budget.

A busy event is not a promise of sales. You still need a reason to participate and a way to be useful.

Arrive with curiosity and something to contribute

Ask what the organization is working on. Learn what a business owner wants to be known for. Offer a relevant resource when you have one. Follow the organizer’s rules and respect the purpose of the gathering.

I want businesses visible in our community because they contribute to it. People should leave a conversation with a clearer understanding of what you do and why it matters to them.

Look beyond a single transaction

A conversation may help you understand a neighborhood, discover a supplier, or learn about a community need. Those are useful outcomes even when they do not turn into an immediate inquiry.

There are also practical resources outside the event circuit. The Florida SBDC at UNF serves Marion County and offers no-cost consulting, including business planning and market research. Explore its published services. A clearer plan can help you decide which opportunities deserve your time.

Make the follow-through specific

If you promised a link, send it. If an introduction makes sense, ask both people first. Mention the conversation so the person understands why you are reaching out. Ask before adding someone to your promotional list.

For your next event, set one practical intention: learn about a local organization, meet a relevant business, or reconnect with someone you can help. Check current details with the organizer before heading out.

A good connection gives both people a reason to keep talking.

Resumen en español

Elige los eventos con un propósito claro. Pregunta por el público, los costos y las responsabilidades antes de participar. Escucha, aporta algo útil y cumple con el seguimiento que prometiste. La visibilidad tiene más sentido cuando también contribuye a la comunidad.
