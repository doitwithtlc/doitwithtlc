A nomination starts with a real connection.

Please nominate a business you do not own, manage or represent. Use your own contact information and explain how you know the business. Self-nominations are not eligible for this process.

We review identity, contact details, social profiles, duplicate submissions and the example you provide. A declaration alone cannot prove independence; our team makes the verification decision.

Submitting does not guarantee publication, an award or gala eligibility. We’ll obtain permission before publishing a business spotlight. Please provide business contact details intended for business inquiries.
