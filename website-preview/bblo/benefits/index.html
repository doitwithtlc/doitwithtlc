What your membership brings together

More ways to learn.
More reasons to connect.

Business workshops & training

Build practical business knowledge through scheduled workshops, presentations, training and conversations with other owners. Bring questions and look for actions you can use in your business.

Relationships that keep growing

Meet regularly, learn what other businesses do and help people understand who you serve. Familiarity gives introductions and follow-up a stronger foundation.

Visibility with a human connection

Introduce your business, get to know the people behind other local businesses and participate in member spotlight opportunities. Your involvement gives others more context when an opportunity comes up.

Community engagement

Learn directly from local nonprofits, participate in community activities and discover where your time, resources and connections can make a difference.

Field trips & firsthand learning

Step outside the usual meeting room through scheduled Field Trip Fridays and local business visits. See how other businesses and community organizations work.

A private member hub

Approved BBLO members have a place for webinar and event updates, photos, videos, member resources and announcements after signing in.

Member partner offers

Explore current C-Suiteware and Media Minds member offers. Confirm eligibility, scope and current pricing through BBLO before purchasing; these services have their own charges.

English & Spanish connections

Build relationships across language and business backgrounds. You do not have to speak Spanish to participate. No tienes que hablar español para unirte.

Programs, topics, presenters and visit locations follow the published schedule. Check each activity’s details for availability and any separate registration cost.

Your membership investment

Make room for your business to grow.

Monthly membership

$40 / month

Recurring on the 1st. A flexible way to make ongoing participation part of your business routine.

$65 non-refundable application fee. Application plus one month: $105.

Annual single membership

$450 / year

Equivalent to $37.50 per month. Save $30 compared with twelve $40 monthly payments.

$65 non-refundable application fee. Application plus annual single membership: $515.

Annual dual membership

$600 / year

Ask BBLO which dual-membership arrangement fits your business and confirm the applicable application requirements.

Your first two visits are free. Drop-ins after those visits are $20 each. Membership requires committee review and final approval; submitting an application or payment does not itself grant member access.
