How to join

Start with a visit.
Build from there.

Come meet local business owners, introduce your business and see how BBLO works.

Your Friday connection.

Regular meetings are Fridays, 8–10 AM at Media Minds in north Ocala. Field Trip Fridays may meet elsewhere. Confirm the upcoming meeting before traveling.

Your first two visits are free. Prepare a 30-second introduction: your name, your business, who you serve and the introduction you’re looking for.

From visitor to member

Get to know us. Then apply.

  1. Visit. Meet the group and introduce your business.
  2. Return and connect. Ask questions, learn about members and discuss the current membership process.
  3. Apply. Complete the current application and payment steps provided by BBLO.
  4. Review and onboarding. Membership is subject to committee review and final approval.
