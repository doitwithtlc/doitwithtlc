About BBLO

Get to know the business.
Build the relationship.

BBLO helps local business owners build connections through member spotlights, practical learning, referrals and consistent participation.

Founded by Carmen Garcia Tirado, BBLO operates under The Liaison Companies LLC. Its bilingual, multicultural mission welcomes qualified business owners and professionals from different backgrounds.
