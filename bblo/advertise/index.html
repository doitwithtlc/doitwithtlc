What your booking includes

Materials, production and approvals

Directory placement uses your supplied logo or photo, a short description and one profile link; one factual or image update per quarter is included. Homepage placement includes template formatting and one consolidated revision, with one creative per booked month.

Stories use your completed written questionnaire and approved facts, in one language. Translation, interviews, photography, video, extra revisions and substantial rewrites require a separate written quote. Stories link from the member profile and a sponsored-story index. Social posts, email distribution, paid media and event recognition are additional services.

Allow five business days for directory or homepage setup after payment and complete materials. Allow ten business days for a story draft, followed by your approval. Confirm actual live dates before booking; the placement or hosting term begins on its agreed live date.

Booking periods and renewals

One business per booking. Annual dual membership does not automatically provide two advertising placements. Monthly placements use calendar months; annual directory placement runs 12 months from the agreed start. No category exclusivity or permanent first position is included.

We confirm each renewal separately; there is no automatic renewal. A story includes 12 months online. Hosting-only renewal is $50 for another 12 months, including one factual correction of up to 15 minutes; a rewrite or new promotional placement is quoted separately. Annual homepage bookings are not offered at launch.

Cancellations and missed delivery

Unstarted placement periods are refundable. Once a monthly period begins, there is no partial-month refund. For annual directory cancellation, delivered months are deducted at the booked monthly rate and the unused balance is refunded. Unearned story-production fees are refundable before publication; no refund follows approved publication. Missed delivery caused by TLC downtime is extended or credited.
