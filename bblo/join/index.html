Your Friday connection.

Regular meetings are Fridays, 8–10 AM at Media Minds in north Ocala. Field Trip Fridays may meet elsewhere. Confirm the upcoming meeting before traveling.

Your first two visits are free. Prepare a 30-second introduction: your name, your business, who you serve and the introduction you’re looking for.

Plan your visitConfirm with Carmen · 352-207-9770

From visitor to member

Get to know us. Then apply.

  1. Visit. Meet the group and introduce your business.
  2. Return and connect. Ask questions, learn about members and discuss the current membership process.
  3. Apply. Complete the online application, select your membership plan and optional advertising interests, then submit.
  4. Pay online. After submission, continue to secure checkout for your selected membership and the one-time $65 application fee.
  5. Review and onboarding. Membership is subject to committee review and final approval.

Membership investment

Make participation part of your visibility plan.

OptionInvestment
Application$65 non-refundable
Monthly membership$40/month · 12-month membership term
Annual membership$450 single / $600 dual · rolling 12 months
Drop-in$20 after the two free visits

Submit your application first, then pay online. New monthly applicants pay $105 initially ($40 dues + $65 application fee); annual single applicants pay $515 ($450 dues + $65 application fee). Annual dual applicants pay $665 initially ($600 dues + one $65 application fee). Dual eligibility remains subject to BBLO review. Payment does not guarantee approval. The application fee is non-refundable; membership dues are refundable only if BBLO denies the application.

Membership application

Complete every step and submit your application. You will then be taken to the membership payment page. Select the same plan and use the same email at checkout so we can match your payment.

Optional advertising interests are saved with your application; placements and advertising payment are confirmed after membership approval.
