What is BBLO?

A place to know the people
behind the businesses.

Bilingual Business Leaders of Ocala is a community and visibility program under The Liaison Companies LLC, founded by Carmen Garcia Tirado. Members get to know one another’s services, share knowledge, make relevant connections and participate in the local business community.

English and Spanish are both welcome. You can participate without being bilingual.

Before you join

A few helpful answers.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. BBLO welcomes English- and Spanish-speaking business owners and professionals. You do not have to be bilingual to participate.

Can I visit before applying?

Yes. Your first two visits are free. Drop-ins after those visits are $20 per visit. Check the calendar and visitor information before attending.

Is my public business profile an extra charge?

Your standard public business profile and directory listing are included with paid BBLO membership. Optional advertising placements are purchased separately.

What do I save by paying annually?

Annual single membership is $450, compared with $480 for twelve $40 monthly dues payments. That saves $30 per year. The $65 application fee is separate in either case.

What does annual dual membership cover?

Annual dual membership is $600. Contact BBLO to confirm the qualifying arrangement and application requirements for the people or businesses involved.

Are Field Trip Fridays always at the regular meeting location?

No. Field trips may meet at another location or start at a different time. Check the individual event listing before traveling.

How do I join?

Start with a visit, then request the current application and payment instructions. Membership is subject to committee review and final approval.
