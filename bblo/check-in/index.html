BBLO Friday Check-in

Visiting BBLO?

Choose Visitor, then select your first or returning visit. Share how you found us, your business, contact details and permission for BBLO/TLC to follow up.

Already a member?

Report completed activity since the last meeting. Zero is a valid total. Include the names of your 1:1 partners and the people connected through your referrals.

For each referral: identify the person who needed the service and the BBLO member you recommended. Choose Facebook shoutout, text, phone call or in person, and indicate whether you notified the member. For additional referrals, use one line per connection in the referral details field, including notification status.

Check-ins are collected privately in BBLO’s CRM. Recording a referral does not send a notification to the recommended member.
