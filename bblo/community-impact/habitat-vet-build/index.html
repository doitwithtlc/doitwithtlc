Community build · July 10, 2026

Habitat Vet Build

BBLO took part in a Habitat Vet Build, connecting local business owners with the work of building homes and supporting our neighbors.

Why it matters

Showing up for a place to call home.

Affordable housing connects to the wellbeing of the whole community. Working alongside Habitat gives business owners a practical way to contribute time, build relationships through shared work and learn where their support can be useful.
