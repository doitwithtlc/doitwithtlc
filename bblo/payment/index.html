Membership payment today

Monthly: $105 · Annual single: $515 · Annual dual: $665

Each includes the $65 one-time, non-refundable application fee. Monthly membership is $40 per month with a 12-month term. Annual single is $450 per year; annual dual is $600 per year. Annual plans cover a rolling 12 months and renew on the anniversary. Dual eligibility is subject to BBLO review.

Optional advertising at checkout

Featured Directory: $15 for one month or $150 for 12 months. Homepage Spotlight: $50 for one month. Sponsored Business Story: $225 one time.

Check only the add-ons you want. Choose one directory duration. Advertising is prepaid for the stated duration and does not automatically renew. Introductory rates apply to first bookings paid by February 6, 2027: up to 12 directory months, 3 homepage months and 1 story per business.

Advertising starts after membership approval and confirmation of availability, dates, scope and terms. It is separate from membership dues.

Application review

Payment does not guarantee membership. BBLO reviews your application and required business documents before a final decision. Membership dues are refundable only if BBLO denies your application.
